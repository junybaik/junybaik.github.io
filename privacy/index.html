Pay Me Right

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version

Pay Me Right does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and no network. Your pay information stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app knows lives in a database on your device:

  • your hourly rate, work state, tax filing status, and pay schedule
  • your Quick Add shortcuts and typical shift length
  • your recurring deductions and any state withholding form you fill in
  • the hours, overtime, tips, and actual deposits you record for each pay period

Nothing is uploaded. The app has no server to upload it to.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or user identifier of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers
  • No crash reporting
  • No internet access at all — every feature works offline

Because there is no account and no identifier, the app cannot associate data with you across devices or reinstalls. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.

Device access

The app requests no device permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. It does not send notifications.

Third parties

None. No third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app.

Taxes and estimates

The app computes tax estimates on your device using published federal and state tax tables stored inside the app. Your pay figures are never sent to a tax service, and no tax preparation or filing is performed.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing their own earnings. It does not deliberately collect information from anyone, of any age, because it collects no information at all.

Your data on your device

If you share or sell your device, someone else could read the app's stored data the same way they could read any other app's data. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app's release notes will say so.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support address listed on the app's store listing.

You can also email paymerightapp@gmail.com or use the support page.
