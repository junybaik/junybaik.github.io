Pay Me Right

Pay Me Right works out roughly what your next paycheck will be — before you get it.

Enter the hours you worked, including overtime, and add any tips or bonuses. Pay Me Right shows an estimate of what should land in your account, and updates it as your hours change. It is built for hourly workers whose pay changes from week to week.

Pay Me Right is free. There is no account to create, no subscription, and no advertising. It works with no internet connection, and your pay information stays on your own device.

These are estimates, not pay statements. Your employer works out the real amounts, and local taxes, benefit deductions, and how an employer rounds can all make an actual paycheck differ.
