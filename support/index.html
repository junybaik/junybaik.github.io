Pay Me Right Support

Pay Me Right has no account and no server, so we cannot look up anything about your use of the app. If something is wrong, tell us what you see and we will help.

Contact us by email

Email: paymerightapp@gmail.com

To write to us:

  1. Open your email app and start a new message.
  2. Address it to paymerightapp@gmail.com.
  3. Describe what you did, what you expected to happen, and what the app showed you instead.

What to include

  • The phone model and system you are using, for example "iPhone 13 on iOS 19".
  • The version of Pay Me Right you have. In the app, open Setup → About Pay Me Right; the version is at the bottom.
  • A screenshot, if something on screen looked wrong. Please hide your rate and pay figures before sending it.

Things worth checking first

Most surprising estimates come from settings rather than from the tax maths. Your hourly rate, recurring deductions, filing status, and work state all feed the estimate, and each is editable in Setup.

If your employer withholds a local tax that Pay Me Right does not calculate, add it as a recurring deduction so the estimate lines up with your real paycheck.

Some states are not supported yet. Where a state's tables are missing, the app says it cannot estimate that withholding rather than showing a figure it cannot stand behind.

Data we cannot recover

Everything Pay Me Right stores lives on your device only. We have no copy of it, so we cannot restore, transfer, or look up anything from a lost, replaced, or reinstalled phone. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.
